# Demo day (Google Meet)

Order: John (Part 1), Ivan (Part 2), Cynthia (Part 3). Every demo is run live
by the person who presents it, on their own computer.

## Hand-offs (only two)

1. The presenter says "Next: <name>" and stops sharing their screen.
2. The next presenter shares their screen at once: IDE already open at the
   right tag, application stopped, console cleared.
3. The slides are driven by the person who is speaking, or by one fixed
   "slide driver" who follows a spoken cue. Agree on one option before the day.

Rehearse both hand-offs with a stopwatch: they must take less than 20 seconds.

## Before you join the call

- Close notifications. Increase the IDE font size (at least 16 pt).
- Check out the starting tag of your part. Run the demo once.
- Have the 60-second backup recording ready to open.

## If a step fails during the demo

1. Say it calmly: "Let me start from the checkpoint."
2. `git checkout <tag of the previous step>`, run.
3. If it still fails, play the backup recording and keep talking.
